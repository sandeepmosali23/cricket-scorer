Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Introduction

Cricket Scorer Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our cricket scoring application.

Information We Collect

Match Data

When you create and score cricket matches, we store:

  • Team names and player names
  • Match scores, statistics, and ball-by-ball data
  • Match timestamps and format details

Storage Options

You can choose between two storage methods:

  • Local Storage: Data stored only on your device
  • Cloud Storage: Data stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore

No Personal Information

We do NOT collect:

  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Physical addresses
  • Payment information
  • Authentication credentials

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected match data to:

  • Provide cricket scoring and statistics features
  • Save and restore your matches
  • Generate match reports and analytics
  • Improve the application's functionality

Data Security

Your match data is stored securely using:

  • Browser local storage (for local mode)
  • Google Firebase Cloud Firestore (for cloud mode)
  • Automatic data expiration after 7 days for cloud-stored matches

Third-Party Services

Firebase (Google)

When using cloud storage, we use Google Firebase services. Please review Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your match data at any time
  • Delete your matches from cloud storage
  • Clear local storage data from your browser
  • Choose between local and cloud storage

Cookies

We do not use cookies. All data is stored in browser local storage or Firebase Cloud Firestore based on your preference.

Children's Privacy

Our application does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app only stores cricket match data that users voluntarily enter.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when changes were made.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
